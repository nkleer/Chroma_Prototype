IDEA 2

Living engravings

Each picture prints in when its moment opens, hatching first and then the full plate. After that it keeps moving gently: the lamps, candles and windows the kit drew glow and flicker on their own clocks, and the view drifts slowly. The light positions are read from the scene code the pictures are drawn from: 56 in the feast and 13 in the station. Nothing is repainted.

IDEA 4

Their own tarot card

The character gets an engraved card that ages with them. It takes their two lead colours as its washes and the combination's name as its title. It sits at the top of the status panel and becomes the life's last card at the end. I drew these four quickly with the art kit as a sketch. The Visuals thread would give each colour its own pose and props.

IDEA 5

Chapter plates

The big turns of a life get a moment of ceremony: a plate turns in like a book page, holds for a breath, and turns away. They're kept rare so they still feel big.

Press a button above to turn a plate.

How it would land without touching v22.3

  • It's all page and picture work: a new style-and-motion layer beside the game's page, plus kit scripts for the light lists and the portrait cards. There are no engine, Library or rate changes, so every life plays out exactly as before.
  • It's built on its own branch while v22.3 is being built, then lands after the v22.3 publish as a page-only update. That needs the short check and the browser drivers, not the full proof.
  • Everything moving can be turned down. It follows the system's reduced-motion setting, and the Table menu gets a Motion switch: full, calm or off.
