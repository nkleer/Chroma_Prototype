# The inner voice and the lessons: modern Earth

Draft for engine v7 (explain.py), Library thread, 2026-10-05. Data: `earth_voice.py`. Checks: `checks_voice.md` (all pass).

Before a choice, the character looks at the options through their own eyes. One line in their own voice describes the moment (the 12 voice keys), and up to two quiet asides from the narrator name what bent it. When heart and head pull apart, one of the lines also says why each side wants what it wants. After the choice come up to three short lessons about how the world works. The engine picks the key; this file gives the words.

- Every key has two lines for any color and the same number for each color: four a color for the 13 keys that come up most (so the player meets repeats less often), two for the rest. 56 keys, 812 lines, and 16 short reasons for heart and head. The engine pools the lines of the character's strongest color with the any-color lines and picks one of them at random (the game has to pass the engine its random stream for that; without it the engine always takes the first line).
- The inner voice speaks in the first person, like Emren's example (it is the first Red line of heart_over_head). The asides and the lessons are in the third person, present tense.
- Each color is a person worth being. White speaks of what is right and owed, Blue reasons and plans, Black is candid about cost and advantage, Red goes with feeling and the moment, and Green trusts belonging and what is meant to be.
- No line names a color, judges a choice or gives a number. A lesson says how something works, never what is right.
- Every line works for a child of four and for an elder. Option labels appear in curly quotes, as the game writes them.

Fills are shown filled in **bold** (Ada is the character). In play the option labels are the library's own and often longer: “bang on their door at two in the morning and have it out”.

## Open points

- The engine now pools the any-color lines with the character's color lines, so they are in play: with n lines a color, each any-color line turns up about once in 2 + n draws. That is also how often a {c} line or a line that gives the reasons is seen.
- Goals, kinds and identities now come in plain words (“the dream of a working life”, “the family”, “becoming principled and thoughtful”), and perk words come as a predicate, so every perk line names the person. Two catalogue says were past tense and now read after the name: “has finished school”, “has come into an inheritance”.
- A few engine words still read oddly. plain_goal's fallback for a goal with no colors, “something of their own”, is third person in the inner voice. After “someone who”, which perk_loss uses, three perk says read awkwardly once lost: “someone who is someone not to cross” (the say's own “someone” comes twice), “someone who has come into an inheritance” and “someone who is a citizen at last”.
- The head's exit reason, “because of all I have already put into this”, is for staying; since the engine's 15:00 hand-off, leaving has its own reason (exit_out, “because staying no longer fits who I am”).
- The lacks aside has not appeared in a run yet: no act in earth.py requires a title or perk so far.
